## 3. Harness

- **Judge tolerance defect (file and fix):** 1-009 trial 3 rejected 3.95% vs
  expected 3.93% with the reason "0.51% … exceeds the 2% tolerance" — 0.51%
  is *inside* the tolerance; trial 1 accepted the same delta. The
  `agent_answer` judge is doing the tolerance comparison itself, against the
  working agreement (numbers in code, semantics to the judge). Move the
  tolerance check into code or pre-compute the pass/fail for the judge. This
  single defect turned a passing row into a majority-fail (trials 1.0/None/
  0.0 → tie → conservative fail).
- **Missing verdicts to reconcile:** several checks show INSUFFICIENT DATA in
  flakiness (`chart_integrity` 263/288, `charts_answer_judge` 196/216,
  `answer_traceability` 215/261) — mostly legitimate n/a (no chart in that
  trial ⇒ chart checks null), which then interacts with tie-breaking: a
  [1.0, None, 0.0] row fails conservatively (1-009, 1-038). Consider whether
  a 1-1 tie with a null third trial should instead surface as "needs rerun".
- **Info-only probation verdicts (first 3-trial evidence):**
  `answer_traceability` std ±0.07 but 46 missing verdicts; ` charts_answer_judge`
  ±0.09, 13 flapping; `class_value_match` mean 0.50. None meet the ≤0.10-std
